Published at ICLR 2026. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.21613)

Studied which types of metadata, beyond URLs, can speed up LLM pretraining. We show that fine-grained metadata, such as document-quality indicators, accelerates training when prepended to documents, and introduce metadata appending, where predicting the metadata as an auxiliary task also improves training efficiency. Using probing, we analyze how metadata shapes the model's internal representations, and derive practical guidelines for using metadata in pretraining.
