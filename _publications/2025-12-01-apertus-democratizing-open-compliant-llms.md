Published at ACL 2026. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.14233)

Apertus is a fully open suite of multilingual LLMs (8B and 70B parameters) trained on 15T tokens from over 1,800 languages, using only openly available, compliance-filtered data. I contributed to the long-context extension of the models and to their evaluation.
