I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at EPFL, in the Machine Learning and Optimization Lab (MLO), advised by Martin Jaggi. I have recently joined the pretraining team at Mistral AI.

My research focuses on making large foundation models more efficient to train and serve, primarily through innovations in **model architecture**. I am interested in addressing this from both an algorithmic and a modeling perspective.

Currently, I focus on:

- **Attention mechanisms:** sparse and efficient attention that reduces the cost of training and inference, especially at long context lengths.
- **Architecture design:** rethinking the core components of the Transformer to improve the trade-off between model quality and computational cost.
- **Efficient large-scale pretraining:** data-centric and communication-efficient strategies for pretraining at scale.

You can find my full background and publications on the [CV page](/cv/).
